About Me

A Few Words About Me

Hi, my name is Long! I was born on June 9th, 2009, and I’m from Hung Yen, Vietnam. I’m currently a student at Trieu Quang Phuc High School in Hung Yen.

Ever since I was young, I have been curious about and passionate about electronic devices and technology.

My greatest passion lies in computers and smartphones—a fascination that dates back to my childhood, when I used to tinker with rooting Android devices just to "hack" games.

Why the passion?

It all started during the COVID pandemic: while studying at home on my crummy laptop, a mix of curiosity and happenstance led me to tinker with ways to reduce computer lag—and that’s where my passion for programming was born.

It all started during my final years of middle school when I first discovered coding through online research; driven by passion and curiosity, I successfully created my own website using a self-purchased domain and hosting.

That’s when my passion began to grow, and I started working on my own projects. However, when it came time for high school entrance exams, I focused entirely on studying and put that passion aside for two years; it wasn't until 12th grade that I picked it up again from scratch.

Hobbies

Aside from programming, I don't really have that many hobbies.

  • Sometimes I exercise or play sports with friends—for example, cycling, playing badminton, or swimming.
  • With a naturally curious nature and a rural upbringing, I enjoyed wild pastimes like fishing and catching small creatures—such as lizards—in the wild to keep as playthings.
  • I really enjoy going out, especially with the important people in my life.
  • I’m also a big fan of traveling and nature, especially quiet.

Strengths and Weaknesses

Strengths:

  • I’m naturally curious and always eager to explore and understand the world around me.
  • When something piques my interest, I dive deep into learning and understanding it wholeheartedly.
  • I’m open, friendly, and enthusiastic in communication, which helps me build meaningful relationships and learn from others.

Weaknesses:

  • I am a very lazy person when it comes to everything.
  • I’m also someone who often gets distracted by random thoughts, losing focus on the main task at hand.

Sometimes I act impulsively—my hands moving faster than my brain—and do things without thinking them through.
